Accessibility statement · Version 1.0 · August 2026

Built to be readable by everyone.

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It uses semantic HTML, works without JavaScript (there is none), supports keyboard navigation with visible focus states, respects reduced-motion preferences, and keeps text contrast at or above AA on every surface — body text runs at 14:1 against the background.

If anything here is hard to use with your setup — screen reader, magnification, keyboard, anything — email us and we will fix it or provide the content another way.
