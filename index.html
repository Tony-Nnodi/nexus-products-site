What we do

Buy genuine stock

We purchase branded products from authorised UK wholesale distributors — invoiced, traceable, and retained on file. We never rename or relabel what we sell.

Sell where customers already shop

Our stock is sold through marketplace storefronts, principally NexusGoods on amazon.co.uk, with fulfilment handled through the marketplace's own logistics.

Keep everything on the record

Our own operations software screens every product against hard rules before we may buy it, caps our spending in code, and writes an audit trail no one can edit.

Why counterparties deal with us

A company you can check
Registered in England and Wales as company 17425320. The details on this site match Companies House, our marketplace accounts and our trade applications — character for character.
Systematic buying
A product must pass a fixed set of evidence rules — demand, competition, margin — before our system permits a purchase order. If the evidence isn't there, we can't buy. Neither enthusiasm nor habit overrides it.
Hard spending controls
Order-size limits and total-exposure caps are enforced in code, not policy documents. A purchase that would break them cannot be raised.
A named person answers
You deal with the director, from a company-domain address, and we answer plainly. We are small — which means nothing gets lost.
